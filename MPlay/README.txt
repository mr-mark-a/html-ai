========================================================
  Mmusic Player for TRIMUI Smart Pro S
========================================================

INSTALLATION INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Copy the entire 'MPlay' folder onto your MicroSD card under:
   /mnt/SDCARD/Apps/MPlay

2. Insert the MicroSD card into your TRIMUI Smart Pro S.

3. Navigate to the "Apps" menu on your console and launch "Mmusic Player".

APP CONTENTS:
- Mmusic.html   : Main music player optimized for 1280x720 16:9 landscape display
- icon.png      : TRIMUI launcher app icon
- config.json   : TRIMUI app config file
- launch.sh     : Shell launcher script
